Privacy Policy

Aarohi Design respects your privacy and handles personal information responsibly. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you when you contact us or use our website.

We may collect your name, phone number, email address, project details, site location, and message content when you submit an enquiry, request a consultation, or communicate with us through phone, WhatsApp, email, or form submissions.

We use this information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, schedule site visits, manage projects, provide design and construction services, and improve our communication with clients. We do not sell your personal information.

We may share limited information with trusted contractors, vendors, consultants, or service partners only when it is necessary to complete a project, fulfill legal requirements, or support approved services. Any such sharing is limited to business needs.

Data Retention and Security

We keep project-related records and communication only for as long as needed for business, legal, or accounting purposes. Reasonable technical and administrative safeguards are used to protect data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

Cookies and Website Usage

Our website may use standard cookies or analytics tools to understand visitor behavior, improve page performance, and enhance the browsing experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings if you prefer.

Your Choices

  • You may request updates to your contact details.
  • You may ask us to stop using your contact information for non-essential communication.
  • You may contact us for clarification about how your information is used.

Contact Information

If you have privacy-related questions about Aarohi Design or this policy, please contact us at info@aarohidesign.in or through the contact details listed on our website.
